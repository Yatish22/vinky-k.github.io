Synopsis
THE STORY

In a bustling world dominated by technology, Prisha Verma stands out — not just for her unparalleled coding skills, but for the enigma she carries. A young woman with a mind as sharp as her wit, she rises through the ranks of TechNovva Solutions, solving impossible challenges and winning hearts.

But beneath her brilliance lies a secret that could shatter everything. When a covert organisation known as Eclipse surfaces, threatening to upend the tech world, Prisha is drawn into a web of deceit, betrayal, and danger.

Her closest allies, Varun and Niya, find themselves questioning everything they know about her as the line between humanity and technology begins to blur.

Who is Prisha Verma? A prodigy? A creation? A saviour? As friendships are tested, truths unravel, and lives hang in the balance, The Coder Girl delivers a gripping tale of innovation, identity, and the boundaries of artificial intelligence.
