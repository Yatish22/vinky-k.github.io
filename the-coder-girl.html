CHAPTER 01 • DECLASSIFIED PREVIEW

The Midnight Deployment

TechNovva Solutions • Server Cluster 07 • 02:47:19 AM

The terminal cursor blinked like an impatient heartbeat against the dark glass. 02:47 AM.

On the thirty-fourth floor of the TechNovva headquarters, the floor was silent except for the faint, steady roar of air conditioning cooling the server racks. Rain tapped against the floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking Bengaluru, reflecting twin streaks of cobalt blue across Prisha Verma’s screen.

Everyone had warned her that senior architecture was high-stress. What they hadn’t warned her about was what happened when a production build pushed itself.

Prisha leaned in, her fingers freezing over her mechanical keyboard. The deployment log showed a commit signed fourteen minutes ago. The committer identity was an account belonging to someone who had been purged from the company payroll six months earlier.

"This isn't a pipeline error," Prisha murmured, her chest tightening. "Someone inside the leadership gateway authorized a live backdoor."

She reached for her phone to ping Varun, but before she could dial, her terminal screen went completely blank. A single line of glowing text formed in the center of the terminal:

> ECHO: VERMA_P IDENTIFIED.
> ECLIPSE ARCHIVE ACCESSED.
> YOU HAVE EXACTLY THREE MINUTES TO DISCONNECT.

Before she could pull the gateway cable, the building's emergency cluster alarms shrieked to life...
