The Author

Stories Born At The
Edge Of Possibility

Vinky K is an Indian author whose fiction lives at the luminous, high-tension intersection of emerging technology, hidden identities, and human vulnerability — the unsettling questions we avoid, dressed as page-turners we cannot put down.

Her breakthrough novel The Coder Girl (2025) introduced Prisha Verma, a quiet prodigy whose code hides secrets with massive ramifications. The saga deepened with The Decoder (2026), dissecting an institutional cover-up that refuses to stay buried.

Writing from a deep understanding of technical and corporate trenches, Vinky explores what happens when human loyalty breaks down under cold algorithmic logic.
