Behind The Ink

Stories Crafted With
Precision & Vulnerability

Vinky K is an Indian fiction author writing at the luminous, high-tension intersection of modern technology, personal identity, and human vulnerability.

Her breakthrough novel The Coder Girl introduced Prisha Verma, a quiet engineering prodigy caught in the gears of a corporate frame-job. The series deepened with The Decoder, dissecting the secrets that powerful organizations bury inside audit logs.

Writing with genuine technical authenticity and cinematic emotional pacing, Vinky explores what happens when human loyalty is pushed to its breaking point.
