The Author

Stories born at the
edge of possibility

Vinky K is an Indian author whose fiction lives at the intersection of technology, identity, and human nature — the unsettling questions we carry, dressed as stories we can't put down.

Her debut novel The Coder Girl (2025) introduced Prisha Verma — a coder whose brilliance hides a secret that could change everything. The series continued with The Decoder (2026), digging deeper into a case that was never truly closed.

Vinky writes about the things that worry us most: what it means to trust, what makes us human, and what happens when the answer turns out to be more complicated than we imagined.

More stories are in progress. The case is still open.
