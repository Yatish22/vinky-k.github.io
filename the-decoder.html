Synopsis

The Case Reopens

Months after the events that shook TechNovva Solutions, the world moved on. The case was closed, the questions buried. But buried things have a way of surfacing.

When investigator Dhruv begins uncovering hidden inconsistencies that don't add up, and coder Vidya stumbles onto clues that were never meant to be found, the forgotten case tears itself back open — more dangerous than before.

At the centre of it all, still and silent, is Prisha. She holds the missing pieces. She always has. But the truth she's been protecting may be the very thing that destroys everything.

In a world built on deception, where artificial intelligence blurs the line between real and constructed, The Decoder asks the question the first book dared not answer: what happens when the truth finally comes out?
