The Author

Writing the stories
that keep you up all night

Vinky K is an Indian author who writes at the intersection of technology, identity, and human nature — the questions that unsettle us, dressed as page-turners that are impossible to put down.

Debut novel The Coder Girl (2025) is a bold, twisting mystery thriller asking one deceptively simple question: what does it mean to be human — and what happens when the answer isn't what you expected?

More stories are coming. The ink is still wet.
